# CrossMux Governance & Community Principles

CrossMux is a community fork of CrossPoint Reader. This document describes
collaboration in `0x1abin/crossmux`; upstream governance, funding programs, and
maintainer assignments are independent of this fork.

## Respectful collaboration

- Assume good intent and allow for differences in language and experience.
- Discuss the code, behavior, and evidence; do not attack contributors.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory conduct are not welcome.
- Keep review comments concrete and explain the user or maintenance impact.

## Proposals and decisions

Report bugs and propose improvements in
[CrossMux Issues](https://github.com/0x1abin/crossmux/issues). Pull requests target
**`0x1abin/crossmux:main`** and follow the
[contribution workflow](./docs/contributing/development-workflow.md).

Maintainers guide acceptance using [SCOPE.md](./SCOPE.md), the hardware budget,
and the long-term maintainability of the reader and its lightweight extensions.
An upstream policy is useful context, but does not automatically define what
CrossMux accepts. Keep technical decisions and their reasoning in issues and
pull requests so other contributors can follow them.

## Review and moderation

Contributors should describe what changed and how it was verified, respond to
review respectfully, and keep each change focused. Maintainers may request
revisions or decline changes that do not meet the project's scope and quality
requirements.

Maintainers may moderate disruptive content, close or lock discussions, and
restrict participation to protect the community. Use GitHub's reporting tools
for abusive content; do not publish private information in a public issue.
