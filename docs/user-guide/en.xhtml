1. Start with this book

Welcome to CrossMux! This short guide helps you start reading. It is an ordinary EPUB: turn its pages, change its font, bookmark it, or delete it just like any other book.

Select a book in Recent books on the home screen and confirm to read it. The file browser finds books on your SD card. Recent books contains books you have opened, plus this guide on first use.

The guide is added automatically only once. Removing its recent entry does not delete the file. Deleting the file does not make it reappear, and firmware updates do not replace your existing copy.

2. Buttons and touch

Follow the action hints on the screen. Confirm opens the selected item, Back leaves a menu, direction buttons move the selection, and Previous page and Next page turn pages.

X3, X4 and other devices have different button locations and capabilities. Button remapping and screen orientation can change their behavior. Check the control settings for your device instead of assuming another model's physical layout.

Only devices with a touch screen support tapping buttons and list items. E-ink refreshes take time: allow the screen to update after an action. An occasional black flash is part of a normal refresh.

3. Add your books

A simple way to transfer books is to turn the device off, remove the SD card, and use a card reader to copy EPUB, TXT or other supported files onto it. Safely eject the card before putting it back. Folders can help organize your books.

For wireless transfer, open File transfer from home and choose a wireless method. Follow the device's connection instructions, then open the address shown on its screen in a browser on a computer or phone on the same network. Leave the transfer screen when finished.

Some devices offer USB Drive mode. Use it only if your device provides that option. Safely eject the drive on the computer before leaving the mode as instructed on the device. Do not remove the card or disconnect power during writes.

After importing a book, find and open it in the file browser. It will then appear in Recent books.

4. Make reading comfortable

Press Confirm while reading to open the reader menu, or use the reading-menu control on a touch device. The available options let you browse chapters, jump to a position, add bookmarks, and adjust fonts, size, spacing and margins.

If characters are missing, check whether your selected font supports the book's language. Follow the font settings instructions to prepare or download additional fonts when needed. Layout changes such as font size can require pagination; give the reader time to finish.

The reader saves your progress normally. Adding this guide to the home screen does not by itself record reading time or mark the guide as read.

5. Return home and explore

Use the return-home option in the reader menu or your device's home action. Close any popup before leaving reading, and follow the current screen's button hints.

Home provides access to files, recent books, transfers, settings and apps. Layouts differ between themes; some themes use tabs.

Settings let you change the interface language, theme and controls. Frontlight, touch and automatic rotation depend on the hardware, so some devices do not offer those options.

6. Common questions

A new book is missing from home? Open it in the file browser first. Recent books is not a list of every book on the SD card.

The first cover or page takes time? The first opening parses the book and builds caches. Please wait; image-heavy or complex books can take longer.

A book will not open? Check that the file is complete and supported, and try a known working EPUB. DRM-protected books require a lawful reading method that supports their protection.

An SD card error appears? Turn off the device and check that the card is inserted correctly. Back up important files and check the card's readability and writability on a computer. Do not format it before making a backup.
