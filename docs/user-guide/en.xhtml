1. Getting Started

The CrossMux Quick User Guide covers the essentials of your first reading session. CrossMux is lightweight reading firmware for ESP32-based e-ink devices, primarily for EPUB, TXT, and other books.

To get started:

1. Insert the SD card and turn on the device.

2. Choose the interface language.

3. Copy books to the SD card.

4. Open a book from Library or Browse Files.

5. Adjust the font, font size, and sleep timeout in Settings.

Do not casually delete /.crosspoint/ from the SD card. It stores reading progress, caches, and some settings.

Official website: https://crossmux.com/

2. Basic Controls

Button positions and counts vary by device. Follow the on-screen hints and your current button mapping:

  • Confirm: open the selected item or enter a menu.
  • Back: return to the previous screen or close a menu.
  • Previous item / page: move or turn backward.
  • Next item / page: move or turn forward.
  • Power: power, sleep, or a shortcut according to the device.
  • Reset: use only if the device has a reset control and stops responding.

Some devices allow button remapping under Settings > Controls. Touch-capable devices also let you tap on-screen controls.

3. Reading Books

CrossMux can browse EPUB, TXT, XTC / XTCH, and image files such as BMP and JPEG. Available formats and controls may vary by device.

While reading, use the mapped page controls to turn pages. Press Confirm to open the reader menu and Back to close it or return.

The reader menu may provide chapters, bookmarks, dictionary lookup, auto page turn, orientation, position jump, and screenshots. Check your device's menu for available options.

The first opening of an EPUB may take longer while pagination data is generated. Later opens are usually faster.

4. Reader Settings

Open Settings > Reader > Text Settings to adjust font, font size, line spacing, margins, and paragraph layout. Look in the reading or display settings for orientation, text anti-aliasing, and image display options.

Changing font or layout settings may require pagination again.

5. E-Ink Refresh

Brief flashing, inversion, or light ghosting during page turns is normal for e-ink displays.

Use Settings > Display > Refresh Frequency to adjust how often a full refresh occurs. If ghosting becomes noticeable, use your device's manual full-screen refresh action.

6. Adding Books

SD Card

Power off and remove the SD card. Use a card reader to copy books onto it, for example into /Books/. Safely eject the card, put it back, and open the book from Library or Browse Files.

Wi-Fi Transfer

Open File Transfer from home and choose an available mode, such as Join Network, Create Hotspot, or Calibre Wireless. After connecting, the device shows a QR code or web address. Open the address on a phone or computer to upload, download, or manage files.

The web file service has no login authentication. Use it only on trusted networks and exit File Transfer when finished.

7. Fonts and Dictionaries

Install fonts from Settings > Reader > Manage Fonts. For manual installation, put font-family folders containing .cpfont files under /.fonts/ or /fonts/ on the SD card, following the font instructions.

Offline dictionaries support StarDict and can be installed from Settings > Reader > Manage Dictionaries.

8. Sleep and Battery Life

Set the idle sleep timeout under Settings > System > Time to Sleep.

For longer battery life:

  • Exit Wi-Fi transfer when finished.
  • Turn off the frontlight or Bluetooth when your device has them and they are not needed.
  • Choose a suitable sleep timeout.

9. Firmware Updates

Only install CrossMux firmware intended for your exact device model. Update information is available at:

  • Website: https://crossmux.com/
  • GitHub: https://github.com/0x1abin/crossmux
  • Releases: https://github.com/0x1abin/crossmux/releases

On devices that support OTA updates, open Settings > System > Check for updates. Do not disconnect power, remove the SD card, or force-reset during an update.

10. Troubleshooting

EPUB Will Not Open

1. Restart the device.

2. Delete this book's cache and reopen it.

3. Copy the EPUB again.

4. Convert the EPUB again with Calibre.

5. Install a matching CrossMux firmware update.

Missing Chinese Characters

Install a .cpfont font containing the required Chinese characters, select it, and reopen the book.

Wi-Fi Network Not Found

Make sure the network uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, and check the password and signal strength.

Device Is Unresponsive

If the device has a Reset control, reset and restart it. If the problem continues, verify that the firmware matches the device model.

11. Important Notes

  • Do not bend or heavily press the e-ink display.
  • Back up important SD-card books and data regularly.
  • Do not casually delete /.crosspoint/.
  • Do not flash firmware intended for another device model.

For more information: https://crossmux.com/
